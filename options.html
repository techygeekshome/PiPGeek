PiPGeek

Picture in picture for any video

How to use it

  1. Open a page with a video and click the PiPGeek icon, or press Alt+P. The main video pops out into a small floating window that stays on top of your other windows.
  2. Click the icon or press the shortcut again to put it back.
  3. To choose one video, right-click it and pick Pop out video. On some sites you need to right-click twice to get past the site's own menu.

PiPGeek picks the biggest video that is playing and on screen, including videos inside frames from the same site and inside page components. If a site has switched picture-in-picture off for its player, PiPGeek switches it back on for that video.

Pop out when I switch tabs

This works on tabs where you have used PiPGeek since the page loaded. The browser only does it while the video is playing with sound, and it may ask you once to allow it. Coming back to the tab puts the video back.

Needs access to the sites you visit, so your browser will ask. PiPGeek only uses it to tell the page it may pop its video out. It does not read or send anything.

Firefox

Firefox has its own picture-in-picture as well. Hover over a video and click the button on its edge, press Ctrl+Shift+], or right-click the video and pick Watch in Picture-in-Picture.

PiPGeek uses the same window. If your version of Firefox does not let add-ons open it, PiPGeek puts a Pop out video button on the video, or shows you the Firefox way. Firefox does not let add-ons pop videos out by themselves when you switch tabs.

Privacy

PiPGeek has no account, no adverts and no tracking, and it sends nothing anywhere. It only looks at a page when you click its icon, press the shortcut or use its right-click menu. The only thing it stores is the setting on this page, on your own computer.
